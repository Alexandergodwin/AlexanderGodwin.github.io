My tech journey started during my penultimate year in college while studying Biomedical Science. At the time, I was trying to figure out which digital skill or business niche I could genuinely see myself growing in. A conversation with a close friend led me to Data Analysis, and what started as curiosity quickly became the best decision I ever made

My foundation in Data Science and AI were strengthened through certifications from Google and IBM—if you’re diving into data science, why not let the tech giants tag along? Basically, I went from “What exactly is Python?” to arguing with missing values and debugging code at 2AM.

In 2024, I joined Excellerate as a Data Analyst Associate Intern, where I led the development of a comprehensive analytics dashboard as Team Lead for the Analytics Team. My role focused on analyzing user participation across the platform, generating actionable insights, and helping drive data-informed decisions. We also tracked user retention and churn patterns to better understand engagement behavior and develop smarter retention strategies—because apparently, data can tell you who’s leaving before they actually leave.

Later that year, I was onboarded as a Data Scientist at Nobel Learning PBC, where I collaborated with the team to develop a machine learning clustering model that grouped interns based on age compatibility using historical data and unsupervised learning techniques. The project contributed to a measurable improvement in intern satisfaction and reduced negative pairing feedback, proving that machine learning can sometimes understand people better than people do.

In 2025, I took a brief pause from actively pursuing tech opportunities to complete my hospital internship program and secure my professional license in healthcare after college. The experience was intense, demanding, and honestly one of the most growth-filled periods of my life. It deepened my understanding of healthcare systems, strengthened my discipline, and reinforced my passion for applying data, AI, and automation to solve real-world healthcare and business problem

Alongside Data Science, I’ve also developed a growing interest in AI Automation, exploring how intelligent workflows and automation tools can simplify repetitive processes, improve productivity, and create scalable solutions for businesses and healthcare systems alike.

These days, I’m focused on building solutions at the intersection of Data Science, AI, Automation, and Healthcare—while still trying to convince myself that one more online course won’t hurt haha.
